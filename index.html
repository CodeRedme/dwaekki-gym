      🛡️ Safety Center

      • Stop if something causes significant or sharp pain.
      • Never exercise through an injury — rest and recover first.
      • Warm up before, cool down after.
      • Use any equipment the way it's meant to be used.
      • Keep clear space around you before you start moving.
      • Don't copy advanced moves just because they look cool online.
      • Ask a qualified adult, doctor, or physiotherapist if something feels off.
      This site is built to be anti-extreme-diet and anti-body-shaming. There is no weight tracker, no calorie counter, and no "before/after" culture here — progress is measured in movement, consistency, and how you feel. 💗
      Dwaekki is a fitness helper, not a medical professional. For pain, injury, fainting, breathing problems, disordered eating, or any medical concern — please talk to a parent/guardian, doctor, physiotherapist, or other qualified professional.

      🔒 Privacy

      • Everything (name, progress, week planner, points) stays on this device.
      • No location, contacts, or personal/health data is collected.
      • Your offline Dwaekki tools (workouts, food ideas, the mission game) run fully on this device.
      • When you use Ask Dwaekki's online AI, your message is sent to the AI service Dwaekki uses (Puter) so it can reply — Dwaekki Gym itself doesn't store that chat history on a server.
