🐷 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Dwaekki Gym is a free, fan-made passion project built for STAYs (fans of Stray Kids) to get a no-pressure home workout and balanced-meal companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JYP Entertainment or Stray Kids in any way.

Your privacy matters, so here's exactly what happens (and doesn't happen) with your data:

What we store

  • Everything you enter in the app — your name, mission progress, workout streaks, food logs, coins, and wallpaper choices — is stored locally on your own device, not on a server we control.
  • You can wipe all of this at any time using the Clear My Data button in Settings.

Google / YouTube Sign-In

If you choose to tap "Sign in with YouTube," Dwaekki Gym asks Google for permission to view your YouTube account (read-only) so it can show you your playlists and liked videos inside the Music tab.

  • We only request read-only access — Dwaekki Gym cannot post, upload, delete, or change anything on your YouTube account.
  • This is entirely optional. If you'd rather not sign in, you can use the platform picker or paste a YouTube link instead — every feature still works without signing in.
  • We do not store your YouTube data on any server. It's used only to display your own playlists/videos back to you inside the app, in your current session.

What we don't collect

  • No location tracking
  • No contacts access
  • No ads, no ad trackers, no analytics that identify you personally
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone
